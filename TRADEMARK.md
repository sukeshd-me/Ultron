## 1. Purpose of This Policy

This Trademark & Branding Policy outlines the permitted and restricted uses of the name **"ULTRON"**, the official logos, iconographic assets, visual identities, and related brand identifiers (collectively, the **"ULTRON Marks"**).

The **ULTRON** software source code is published under the open-source **MIT License** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The MIT License grants broad permissions to study, modify, and distribute the underlying software code. **However, the MIT License does NOT grant trademark rights, brand rights, or rights to use the project's identity.**

This policy balances two fundamental goals:
1. Enabling the open-source community to freely use, modify, and develop software with ULTRON.
2. Protecting users and developers by preventing confusion, misrepresentation, misleading endorsements, or deceptive distributions that claim to be "Official ULTRON".

## 2. Protected Marks & Brand Assets

The following marks and creative brand assets are strictly reserved by the project owner:

- The name **"ULTRON"** when used in connection with computer software, desktop applications, artificial intelligence command centers, operating system tools, or related technological services.
- The product title **"ULTRON Personal AI Command Center"**.
- The official **ULTRON logo**, logomark, typography treatments, and icon designs.
- The distinct visual HUD identity, including the official 3D particle core branding graphics (`assets/ultron_social_preview.jpg` and application icon assets).
- Any confusingly similar variations, acronyms, or phonetic spellings (e.g., "UltronAI", "Ultron Desktop", "Official Ultron").

## 3. Permitted Uses (No Prior Permission Required)

You may use the ULTRON name without prior written permission in the following contexts, provided that your use complies with the restrictions in Section 4:

### A. Nominative & Truthful Reference (Fair Use)
- **Truthful Descriptions**: You may refer to the name "ULTRON" to accurately indicate that your software, plugin, script, or tool interfaces with, runs on, or was developed for ULTRON (e.g., *"Custom Wi-Fi plugin for ULTRON"* or *"ULTRON-compatible toolset"*).
- **Articles & Reviews**: You may use the name in technical articles, tutorials, blog posts, YouTube reviews, academic papers, and social media commentary discussing the project.
- **Linking to the Official Project**: You may use the word mark to link to the official repository at `https://github.com/sukeshd-me/Ultron`.

### B. Distributing Unmodified Official Binaries
- You may distribute verbatim, unmodified installer packages (`ultron-setup-1.0.0.exe`) downloaded directly from the official [GitHub Releases page](https://github.com/sukeshd-me/Ultron/releases), provided you do not charge a fee specifically for the mark or package it with adware, spyware, or third-party toolbars.

## 4. Prohibited & Restricted Uses (Prior Authorization Required)

Unless you have obtained prior written authorization from the project owner, the following uses are strictly prohibited:

### A. False Affiliation, Endorsement, or Sponsorship
- You may not use the ULTRON Marks in any manner that implies or suggests that your software, service, company, or fork is endorsed by, affiliated with, sponsored by, or officially approved by the ULTRON project or Sukesh D.

### B. Modified Forks & Rebranding Requirement
- **If you distribute a modified version of ULTRON (either source code or compiled binary packages), you MUST rebrand it.**
- You must remove the official ULTRON logo, icons, and visual branding assets from the binary distribution and documentation.
- You must choose a distinct, non-confusing name for your fork (e.g., *"MyProject (based on the open-source ULTRON codebase)"* instead of *"ULTRON Plus"*, *"ULTRON Pro"*, or *"ULTRON Extended"*).
- Your documentation must clearly state that your fork is an unofficial, independent modification and is not supported by the original ULTRON maintainers.

### C. Commercial Trademarks & Domain Registrations
- You may not register or attempt to register any trademark, service mark, business name, or corporate entity incorporating the word "ULTRON" in the field of AI software or computer automation.
- You may not register domain names incorporating the mark (e.g., `ultron-official.com`, `ultronai.org`) without written consent.

### D. Merchandise & Commercial Brand Exploitation
- You may not manufacture, market, or sell merchandise (apparel, hardware stickers, novelty goods) bearing the official ULTRON logo or branding without written authorization.
